Keeps the last ~20 m around you. Walls not seen again fade in about 25 s. Walking distance comes from LiDAR scan matching; along long plain walls it cannot be measured and the map may stretch.

Camera lens check

When the laser and a camera see the same thing, their two directions are compared. The guide assumes a 70° lens.
