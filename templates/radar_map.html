Uncalibrated boards — live range

These are streaming right now, but they have no measured bearing, so there is no honest place to draw them on the ring above. Each bar is distance only. Wave a hand at one and watch which bar jumps — that tells you which board it is.

Cardinals (what guidance uses)

Six boards reduce to four cardinals, nearest-wins. Front and back each take the nearer of the two boards straddling them; right and left have one board apiece, sitting on their axis.

Calibrate

Put a hand ~1.5 ft in front of one board. The row whose distance drops is that board — set its bearing, then Save.

The options are the six bearings the mount actually has (30/90/150/210/270/330). There is deliberately no 0°: no board points straight ahead — 30° and 330° straddle it, and together they are front.

Boards

The A121 measures range only — it has no angle resolution inside its beam. Each return is drawn as an arc across the whole wedge because the object could be anywhere along it. A dot would imply a bearing the sensor cannot measure.
